# Hjulben – Official Trails Plan

A plan for a career of 35 official trails, where every trail brings in something new and the difficulty climbs steadily. How the career is designed, and the rules every trail follows, are in [TRAILS_GUIDE.md](TRAILS_GUIDE.md). Once every trail here is built, this plan can go.

---

## Where we are

We keep **The Orchard** (1) as it is, and **Apple Valley** (2) and **Windfall** (3) are built. **Splinter Ridge** is kept but moves to 6, where its cut-wood look opens the Woods. The other current official trails (High Hopes, Skybound, Brake Point, Long Way Up and Elastic Summit) are retired. Their best ideas are reused below, and two of the names come back as rebuilt trails.

Until the new trails exist, the files in `trails/official/` run 01 The Orchard, 02 Apple Valley, 03 Windfall, 04 A Hill to Die On, 05 Splinter Ridge, and then the old trails from 06 to 10.

---

## The 35 trails

Names are working titles. "Also new" is the visual element introduced alongside the riding element.

### World 1 – First Rides
*Grass, `hills` backdrop, the orchard and the farm around it, noon, clear skies. Teaches the controls.*

- [x] **1. The Orchard:** gas, lean and gentle rollers. *Also new:* farm props, collecting apples.
- [x] **2. Apple Valley:** momentum. Rollers grow from small to large, then a deep dip in front of a big hill shows that a run-up carries you up. The flower hangs over the dip past a small kicker, so the trail ends with a first little hop. Nothing is steeper than 33°, and the bot finishes in 11.21 s (gold 14). *Also new:* the scarecrow, beehive, ladder and tyre.
- [x] **3. Windfall:** turning around. See [Turning around](#turning-around) below. The bot finishes in 28.02 s with a scripted opening (gold 32). *Also new:* windfall apples in the grass, hay bales.
- [x] **4. Hay Lane:** braking. A downhill run to a fence, with a `SLOW` sign before a drop. *Also new:* signs, tyre barriers.
- [x] **5. Windmill Hill (finale):** steep climbs. Lean forward to keep the front down, and if you stall, roll back, turn and try again with a run-up. Combines momentum, turning and braking. *Also new:* morning light, more clouds, the farmhouse.

### World 2 – The Woods
*Dirt, `forest` backdrop, cut wood, morning to evening. Teaches balance.*

- [x] **6. Splinter Ridge (moved from 2):** steeper rollers and sharper crests. Ease off over the top, and lean forward when the front wheel gets light. *Also new:* dirt, the forest backdrop, pines, cut stumps, roots and moss.
- [x] **7. Kickback:** wheelies. Steep faces lift the front wheel, and leaning controls it. *Also new:* evening light.
- [x] **8. Stoppie Slope:** steep descents of 45° or more, leaning back so you don't go over the bars. Reuses ideas from Brake Point. *Also new:* vines over cliffs.
- [x] **9. Stump Drops:** small drops and step-downs, landing rear wheel first. *Also new:* the squirrel, stone dike props.
- [x] **10. Switchback (finale):** turn around again and again to climb to a tree house, zig-zagging up between ramps. Combines wheelies, steep descents, drops and turning around. *Also new:* light rain, a tree house at the top.

### World 3 – The Desert
*Sand, `desert` backdrop, bright noon with sun glare. Teaches air.*

- [x] **11. Dune Hopper:** first air off rounded crests, and using lean to level the bike in the air. *Also new:* sun glare, cacti.
- [x] **12. Kicker Canyon:** kickers, ramps that throw you up, onto downhill landings. *Also new:* boulders, crystals.
- [x] **13. Sandpit:** pits with a floor. A short jump is costly but you can recover. Reuses ideas from Skybound. *Also new:* pebbles, the skull and car wreck props.
- [x] **14. Mind the Gap:** the first open gaps, 80 → 90 → 100 units wide. Falling in ends the run. *Also new:* warning signs and tyre walls before each gap.
- [x] **15. Mirage (finale):** apples floating over gaps, and a floating island to jump onto and off. *Also new:* heat haze.

### World 4 – Lakeland
*Grass and dirt, `hills` and `mountains` backdrops, morning, rain. Teaches water, and turning around in the air.*

- [x] **16. Wet Feet:** shallow fords that drag on the bike and slow it down. *Also new:* reeds, lilies.
- [x] **17. Deep End:** deep water wipes you out. The first pool has a dry way round, and the second must be cleared with a small hop. *Also new:* ducks, fish, the floating rider after a crash.
- [x] **18. Jetty Jump:** jumping over wide deep water. *Also new:* moderate rain, jetty and rowing boat props.
- [x] **19. Boomerang:** turning around in the air. Jump off a kicker, turn while airborne, and land facing back onto a ledge above where you took off. *Also new:* a frog on a lily pad.
- [x] **20. Out and Back (finale):** ride out, collect, turn around, and finish behind where you started. Combines fords, deep water and turning around. *Also new:* distant thunder.

### World 5 – Underground
*Rock, back walls, darkness. Teaches tight spaces.*

- [x] **21. Low Bridge:** overhangs and low ceilings. Keep your head down and don't bounce. *Also new:* hanging roots, stalactites.
- [x] **22. Lantern Mine:** the first dark cave, closed off by a back wall. Ride by the headlight and lanterns. *Also new:* the headlight, minecarts, beams, drips.
- [x] **23. Sinkhole:** drop from the surface into a cave and climb out of a second exit. The trail has two levels. *Also new:* bats, glowing mushrooms.
- [x] **24. The Shaft:** start at the bottom of a cave shaft and climb out by switchbacking between ledges on alternate sides, up to a finish that floats high above. *Also new:* a start below ground, a floating finish.
- [x] **25. The Ring (finale):** the first loop. Needs the loop handling pass in `PHYSICS.md` first. Combines ceilings, darkness and climbing. *Also new:* a large cavern lit by lanterns.

### World 6 – The City
*Brick, `city` backdrop, evening to night. Teaches hazards.*

- [x] **26. Kerbside:** step-ups. Pop the front wheel up brick stairs and kerbs with near-vertical faces and crisp corners. *Also new:* cones, barriers, graffiti.
- [x] **27. Killer Street:** spikes. First a still spike well off the route, then still spixkes on the route with plenty of room. *Also new:* dumpsters, pigeons.
- [x] **28. Spin Cycle:** spinning spikes, and spikes hanging under ledges, so you must control how high you go. *Also new:* scaffolding in front of the rider.
- [x] **29. Night Shift:** the first full night. Street lamps light the route, and apples can only be seen where light reaches them. *Also new:* lit windows, crane lights.
- [x] **30. Rooftops (finale):** gaps between buildings, with spikes and night. *Also new:* sponsor banners, the halfway flag.

### World 7 – The Mountains
*Snow and rock, `mountains` backdrop, worsening weather. The final tests.*

- [x] **31. Long Way Up (rebuilt):** endurance. An 800+ unit climb where you must not lose momentum. *Also new:* light snow (`snow` ≈ 0.3), cairns.
- [x] **32. Whiteout:** fog limits how far ahead you can see, so signs and props mark the route. Keep `fog` ≤ 0.5. *Also new:* heavier snow, the ski lift.
- [x] **33. Big Air:** big kickers and gaps of 120–160 units. Air flips are optional, for gold times. *Also new:* a clear high-altitude sky.
- [x] **34. Thunderhead:** a night storm where lightning lights up the route between flashes. *Also new:* lightning, heavy rain.
- [x] **35. Elastic Summit (rebuilt, finale):** the final exam, with something from every world. *Also new:* the summit flag and a full storm.

## Beyond 35: elements that need engine work

The list above uses only features the game supports today, so we run out of new riding elements at around 35. These would open up further worlds:

- [ ] **Grip per material:** today every material has the same wheel friction. Ice, mud and loose sand with their own grip would make a whole winter or swamp world.
- [ ] **Checkpoints:** restart from the halfway flag. Needed for long trails of 5000+ units.
- [ ] **Moving platforms and see-saws:** terrain that moves along a fixed path. It must stay deterministic for replays.
- [ ] **Gates and switches:** touch a switch to open a wall elsewhere.
- [ ] **Optional collectibles:** a hidden nisse in each trail, for replay value without blocking the finish.

## Still to do

1. **Props come first.** Many "also new" items are still unticked in `PROPS_PLAN.md`: signs, tyre walls, hay bales, the jetty, the skull, heat haze, the halfway flag, the ski lift and cairns. Build each world's props before its trails.
2. **Existing saves.** Career progress is stored as an index (`unlocked` in `js/storage.js`), so replacing trails 3–7 changes what existing saves have unlocked. Either accept it before launch, or map old saves to the new order once, when the save is loaded.
3. **Dress the finales.** None of the seven finales has its trials-event props yet (see rule 6 in [TRAILS_GUIDE.md](TRAILS_GUIDE.md)). Props aren't part of the trail's hash (`trailHash` in `js/trail-hash.js`), so adding them keeps a finale's best times, leaderboard and replay.
