# Changelog

Player-facing release notes. The game shows them under **What's new**, and each GitHub Release uses its section.

## 0.6.7 — 2026-11-14

### Heads up
- The game now needs Safari 17.5, Chrome 123 or Firefox 120 or newer.
- Controller rumble and phone vibration are now one Vibration setting. If your controller stopped rumbling, switch Vibration on in Settings.

### New
- Light and dark themes for the page around the game and the trail editor. They follow your device, or pick one in Settings.
- A new bonus trail, Proving Ground I.
- In the editor, Esc leaves for the game: once there's nothing left to back out of, press it twice.

### Improved
- The wheels throw up the ground they ride on: thick roosts on sand, floating powder on snow, clods of dirt. A head-first crash throws it up too, and tyres leave fading tracks in snow and sand.
- On the Pocket Classic you can see the rider's far leg pedalling, and the bikes cough a puff of smoke when the kick-start catches.
- Settings are quicker to read: on/off settings are switches, and a few have a short note on what they do. Reduced scenery is now called Performance mode; your choice is kept.
- Tidier menus, with shorter headers and the icon beside each title. The Garage shows your rider's name and save slot, and its undo and redo buttons stay in place.
- In the editor, the scale handles snap like dragging does, sit snugly around curved blocks, and stretching one side no longer shifts the other.
- In the editor, the finish can float past either end of the terrain, for a last jump to reach it.

## 0.6.3 — 2026-11-09

### Heads up
- High Orchard, the First Rides finale, is now a whole trials day in eight sections that tests everything the world teaches, and ends through a tunnel in the Woods. Its times start afresh.

### New
- The Garage has new accessories and extra cosmetics, undo and redo, and a room that grows with your career. New gear is tagged NEW, and locked items show how close you are to earning them.
- Picking a different bike in the Garage now lets you hear its engine, or the Pocket Classic's bell.
- Earning an achievement or new Garage gear plays a sound.
- Editor: There is a mini map of the whole trail, showing the riding line, the apples, spikes, start, finish and selection, and a dot for each problem. Click or drag it to move around, click a dot to go to the problem, and press M to hide it.
- Editor: Blocks and props can be grouped into one named thing with Cmd+G, such as a house, which selects, moves, copies, flips and locks as one. Double-click to work on one part of it.
- Editor: Blocks can be flipped (X, and Shift+X upside down), and blocks and props can be brought to the front or sent to the back.

### Improved
- Medals on 23 official trails now follow the replay bot's times, so gold is a real challenge. Elastic Summit gets its first medals. Leaderboards, ghosts and best times stay.
- Achievements and gear are now spread out more, so one good run earns one or two rather than a pile at once.
- The Garage is zoomed out, with a real light switch on the wall, a larger career map and a pegboard.
- Your Ghost of best run choice now goes with your save and syncs across devices. New online riders are asked to pick Mine or World.
- Clouds now drift in the wind, including on the menu, and stay still with reduced motion.
- Flowers, vines, sponsor banners, hanging lanterns, the crane's load, tent door flaps and the scarecrow's straw now move in the wind like the grass and flags do, and vines and lanterns swing after a hard landing nearby.
- Marshals drop their flag and the camera operator ducks when you crash nearby, like the spectators.
- Spectators, marshals, the camera operator and the prison guard turn their heads to watch you ride by, and look up as you jump over them.
- The scarecrow, snowman, prisoners and skeleton look up and down at you as well as sideways.
- Editor: Anything you drag snaps to the edges, middles and points of other things nearby and to the grid, with a guide showing what it lined up with. Hold Cmd/Ctrl mid-drag to move freely, press N to turn snapping off or on, and hold Shift to keep a move straight across, up and down, or at 45°.
- Editor: The inspector names what is selected the way warnings do (Block 3, Point 2), puts related fields such as X and Y on one line, and can split an edge in two. A block's width and height can be typed, and X and Y place it by its top-left corner.
- Editor: Letting go of Alt mid-drag now drops the copy and moves the original again, as in Illustrator or Figma.
- Editor: A trail only shows as edited once you let go of a drag.
- Editor: A block's edges can only be dragged once it is selected, and the point or edge you would grab is highlighted, so outlines are no longer reshaped by accident. Escape cancels a drag.
- Editor: Each tool's hint is a single line, and the shortcuts dialog (press ?) is easier to scan, with one line per shortcut and only your machine's modifier shown.
- Editor: The Water tool draws water by dragging, with no size to set first.

### Fixed
- Editor: Tool shortcuts keep working after you click a button or choose from a dropdown.

## 0.6.2 — 2026-11-05

### New
- Each bike now has its own engine sound. The Elasto keeps its two-stroke, while the Scrambler gets a deeper four-stroke single that thumps, lopes and barks. Only the sound changes, and every bike still rides the same.

### Improved

## 0.6.0 — 2026-11-02

### Heads up
- Leaderboards, ghosts and best times have started fresh, because of the changes to how runs start and how finishes are timed.

### New
- Sky Ladder is now trail 6 and Floating Fields is trail 8, both teaching floating islands in First Rides.
- Windmill Hills joins First Rides as trail 5, with a row of tall, thin hills to fly over. High Orchard moves to trail 9 as the world's finale.
- Copenhagen Rooftops joins The City as trail 36.
- The rider now kick-starts the engine when you open a trail, and taps the gear lever with a clunk when setting off from a standstill. Retries start straight away.

### Improved
- The bike now settles into its suspension and starts at rest, so it no longer bounces and waiting gains nothing.
- Finish times are now measured within the step, so close runs no longer tie.
- The clock starts on your first input, or when the bike moves off its start by itself, so a start in the air is timed from the drop.
- Holding gas on the brakes now revs the engine, for a harder pop when you let go.
- Several trails now start resting on level ground, including The Orchard, Windmill Hill, Splinter Ridge, Kicker Canyon, The Shaft, The Ring, The Bonny Tyler and The Mining Cave.
- Rain and snow now fall from the top of the world, so anything overhead shelters what is under it.
- Your helmet now stays on in nearly all crashes at normal trail speed and only comes off in hard head hits.
- Editor: The start now snaps onto the ground beneath its wheels when you place or drag it close to the ground.
- Editor: Validation now warns about points that sit on a straight line between their neighbours and add nothing to the shape.

### Fixed
- Opening a link to one of your own older saved trails now finds it instead of saving a duplicate.
- The backdrop hills and sky no longer sink away on trails that sit far below the usual ground.

## 0.4.0 — 2026-10-18

### Heads up
- The bike physics have been reworked, so existing trail leaderboards start fresh and earlier ghosts and runs won't carry over. Shared trail links will update to the newer version.

### New
- Bike handling now feels more authentic. Suspension uses rising-rate springs, braking pushes back on the frame so the nose dives in proportion to how hard the brakes work, and gas and brake in the air now spin the bike like a real one. Wheel spin also runs down in the air and after a crash, and leaning is snappier.
- New official trails have been added, from Hay Lane to Long Way Up, along with a wood terrain material and trails with real day cycles.
- There's a new weather system, plus wind that sways flags, props and trees, with leaves falling in heavy wind. Rain and snow also move correctly as the camera goes up and down.
- New riding gear to unlock, including the Scrambler bike, new helmets, beards for male riders, new headwear and Bonnie Tyler hair. Plenty of other helmets and the classic bike have been improved, and there's something to find hidden in the trails.
- Editor: Lots of new props, an Objects tab to lock groups or single items, block merging, a quick pick with Cmd/Ctrl+P, trail preview thumbnails, validation warnings that jump to the problem, and shareable custom trail links. Read-only trails can now only be viewed.

### Improved
- The engine sound now follows the real engine, and the old made-up 5-speed gearbox is gone. There are also new skid sounds that vary by surface, suspension clunks and tyre squish.
- The main menu has been redesigned, with a new title font, credits and tabs for the gamepad settings.
- New touch control buttons, a new achievement pop-up and a nicer trail completed card, which stays steady while your world rank loads.
- Apples no longer bob. They hold still with a shine sweep, so the hitbox is exactly the drawn apple.

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-03
