# Hjulben – Props Plan

A plan for the props to add to each level theme, plus guidelines for making them feel alive.

---

## 🇸🇪 Swedish countryside
*Fits the name Hjulben.*

- [ ] **Red cottage**: falu red with white trim, used as a background landmark. The windows light up at night through the time-of-day system.
- [ ] **Dala horse**: for example as a large roadside statue. It could also be a hidden collectible or easter egg.
- [ ] **Midsummer pole**: with flower wreaths, swaying a little in the wind.
- [ ] **Moose crossing sign**: the yellow triangle. It's easy to draw and people recognise it instantly.
- [ ] **Mailbox on a post**: has a small flag that flips up when the bike passes.
- [ ] **Rune stone**: for mountain or forest levels.
- [ ] **Wooden fence**: made of diagonal slats and repeatable along the ground.
- [ ] **Hay drying rack**: hay hung on poles, giving a distinctive Nordic silhouette.

## 🍎 Orchard / farmland

- [ ] **Apple crates**: stacked in piles. They tie in with the apples you collect.
- [ ] **Ladder leaning on a tree**
- [ ] **Wheelbarrow**
- [ ] **Scarecrow**: turns its head as you ride past.
- [ ] **Beehive**: with a few pixel bees drifting around it.
- [ ] **Tractor tyre**: half-buried in the ground, as a nod to the wheel in the logo.

## 🏜️ Desert
*Goes with the existing cactus and sand.*

- [ ] **Cow skull**
- [ ] **Tumbleweed**: rolls slowly and is purely visual.
- [ ] **Barrel cactus**: a smaller cactus variant.
- [ ] **Rusted car wreck**: half-buried in sand.
- [ ] **Wooden water tower**
- [ ] **Windmill water pump**: the blades spin with the wind.
- [ ] **Heat haze**: shimmers near the horizon and is turned off when reduced motion is on.

## 🏔️ Mountain / snow

- [ ] **Trail cairn**: stacked stones that also work as a route marker.
- [ ] **Ski-lift pylon**: with a slowly moving chair.
- [ ] **Snowed-in signpost**: with direction arrows.
- [ ] **Icicles**: wall props on overhangs. They suit the new cave and overhang terrain.
- [ ] **Snow-covered pine tree**: drops a puff of snow after a hard landing nearby.
- [ ] **Hut with a smoking chimney**: looks good at dusk.

## 🏙️ City

- [ ] **Traffic cones**
- [ ] **Crowd-control barriers**
- [ ] **Dumpster**
- [ ] **Graffiti**: wall props, with the Hjulben logo hidden in some of them.
- [ ] **Street lamp**: turns on at night and lights a pool on the ground.
- [ ] **Pigeons**: fly off when the bike comes close.
- [ ] **Construction crane**: in the background.
- [ ] **Scaffolding**: in the foreground.

## 🕳️ Caves and underground
*For the new terrain.*

- [ ] **Hanging roots**: builds on the roots already started.
- [ ] **Stalactites**
- [ ] **Glowing mushrooms**
- [ ] **Mine cart on rails**: wall prop.
- [ ] **Wooden support beams**: wall prop.
- [ ] **Dripping water**: falls one pixel at a time.
- [ ] **Lantern**: lights up the surrounding cave wall.
- [ ] **Bats**: scatter when you ride in.

## 🏁 Motocross track and event props
*These suit every level and make it feel like a trials event.*

- [ ] **Tyre walls**: stacked tyres in different colours.
- [ ] **Hay bale barriers**: placed along dangerous drops.
- [ ] **Course tape and stakes**: strung between two points to mark the line.
- [ ] **Sponsor banners**: made-up brands such as *Knobbly Tyres*, *Äpple Oil* and *Hjulben Racing*.
- [ ] **Spectators**: cheer, or duck when you crash nearby.
- [ ] **Pit board with a number**
- [ ] **Petrol can**
- [ ] **Marshal flags**: the start marshal waves a green flag, and a checkered flag is waved at the finish.
- [ ] **Camera tower**: a small wink at the replay system.

## 🧭 Props that help gameplay
*Decoration that teaches the player without text.*

- [ ] **Warning signs**: before steep drops or spikes, using a skull, a ⚠ or a "!" board.
- [ ] **Arrow signs**: point the way on routes that double back or loop.
- [ ] **Halfway flag**: lights up when passed and matches the split times.
- [ ] **Graffiti-style tutorial drawings**: in The Orchard, showing the controls (lean back, flip, wheelie).
- [ ] **"Missed apple" hint**: a basket of apples near a hidden route.

## 💡 Tips for making props feel alive

1. **Reactions keep the levels lively.**
   Bending grass, a flipping mailbox flag, birds flying off and falling snow make the bike feel like part of the world.
   > ⚠️ Keep these reactions out of the physics simulation so replays still play back exactly the same.

2. **Tie props to existing systems.**
   - **Time of day:** lamps, windows, lanterns
   - **Weather:** puddles and dripping roofs in rain, bright flashes in lightning
   - **Materials:** snowy and sandy versions of the same prop

3. **Use variants instead of new props.**
   One fence with 3 colour palettes and 2 levels of wear gives a lot of variety for little work.

4. **Anchor props to the ground.**
   With the new block terrain, wall props (icicles, graffiti, roots) can attach to the nearest edge and follow its angle. That way they stay in place when you edit the terrain.
