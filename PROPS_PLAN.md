# Hjulben – Props Plan

A plan for the props to add to each level theme, plus guidelines for making them feel alive. See [PROPS_GUIDE.md](PROPS_GUIDE.md) for how to build them.

---

## 🇩🇰 Danish countryside
*Fits the name Hjulben.*

- [ ] **Half-timbered farmhouse**: dark beams, white or ochre-yellow walls and a thatched roof, used as a background landmark. The windows light up at night through the time-of-day system.
- [ ] **Dannebrog flagpole**: a white garden flagpole with the red-and-white flag flying in the wind. The flag could go up when a checkpoint is reached.
- [ ] **Nisse**: a small Danish gnome with a red hat, hidden somewhere in each level as a secret collectible or easter egg.
- [ ] **Sankt Hans bonfire**: the midsummer bonfire with a witch on top. It glows and gives off sparks on night levels.
- [ ] **Mailbox on a post**: in Danish post-red, with a small flag that flips up when the bike passes.
- [ ] **Rune stone**: for mountain or forest levels, inspired by the Jelling stones.
- [ ] **Stone dike**: a low wall of stacked fieldstones (*stendige*) that repeats along the ground.
- [ ] **Round straw bales**: wrapped in white plastic and scattered across fields. They also work as obstacles to ride over.
- [ ] **Self-serve roadside stall**: a small wooden stand with an honesty box and a hand-painted sign like "KARTOFLER 20 KR".
- [ ] **Dolmen**: a Stone Age burial chamber made of large stones with a capstone on top. It works as both a prop and a natural jump.
- [ ] **Wind turbines**: on the horizon, with blades turning slowly.

## 🧭 Props that help gameplay
*Decoration that teaches the player without text.*

- [x] **Warning signs**: before steep drops or spikes, using a skull, a ⚠ or a "!" board.
- [x] **Arrow signs**: point the way on routes that double back or loop.
- [x] **Halfway flag**: lights up when passed and matches the split times.
- [x] **Graffiti-style tutorial drawings**: Showing the controls (lean back, flip, light etc).
- [ ] **"Missed apple" hint**: a basket of apples near a hidden route.

## 💡 Tips for making props feel alive

1. **Reactions keep the levels lively.**
   Bending grass, a flipping mailbox flag, birds flying off and falling snow make the bike feel like part of the world.
   > ⚠️ Keep these reactions out of the physics simulation so replays still play back exactly the same.

2. **Tie props to existing systems.**
   - **Time of day:** lamps, windows, lanterns
   - **Weather:** puddles and dripping roofs in rain, bright flashes in lightning
   - **Materials:** snowy and sandy versions of the same prop

3. **Use variants instead of new props.**
   One fence with 3 colour palettes and 2 levels of wear gives a lot of variety for little work.

4. **Anchor props to the ground.**
   With the new block terrain, wall props (icicles, graffiti, roots) can attach to the nearest edge and follow its angle. That way they stay in place when you edit the terrain.
