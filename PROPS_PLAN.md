# Hjulben – Props Plan

A plan for the props to add to each level theme. How to build them, and how to make them feel alive, is in [PROPS_GUIDE.md](PROPS_GUIDE.md). Once every prop here is built, this plan can go.

---

## 🇩🇰 Danish countryside
*Fits the name Hjulben.*

- [ ] **Half-timbered farmhouse**: dark beams, white or ochre-yellow walls and a thatched roof, used as a background landmark. The windows light up at night through the time-of-day system.
- [x] **Dannebrog flagpole**: a white garden flagpole with the red-and-white flag flying in the wind. The flag could go up when a checkpoint is reached.
- [x] **Nisse**: a small Danish gnome with a red hat, hidden somewhere in each level as a secret collectible or easter egg.
- [ ] **Sankt Hans bonfire**: the midsummer bonfire with a witch on top. It glows and gives off sparks on night levels.
- [ ] **Mailbox on a post**: in Danish post-red, with a small flag that flips up when the bike passes.
- [ ] **Rune stone**: for mountain or forest levels, inspired by the Jelling stones.
- [ ] **Stone dike**: a low wall of stacked fieldstones (*stendige*) that repeats along the ground.
- [ ] **Round straw bales**: wrapped in white plastic and scattered across fields. They also work as obstacles to ride over.
- [ ] **Self-serve roadside stall**: a small wooden stand with an honesty box and a hand-painted sign like "KARTOFLER 20 KR".
- [ ] **Dolmen**: a Stone Age burial chamber made of large stones with a capstone on top. It works as both a prop and a natural jump.
- [ ] **Wind turbines**: on the horizon, with blades turning slowly.

## 🌲 Forest

- [x] **Squirrel**: nibbles a nut, and runs up the nearest tree to hide in the crown when the bike comes close.
- [x] **Coda (corgi)**: a tri-colour Pembroke who potters about, sits and sniffs, and runs over to greet the rider with a woof; `E` makes him sit and pant.
- [x] **Tent**: glows from the lantern inside after dark.
- [x] **Fireplace**: a ring of stones round burning logs, sparks and smoke, lighting up the ground.
- [x] **Grass**: sways in the wind and bends flat as the bike brushes through.
- [x] **Ivy**: a wall prop, climbing a rock face as high as it goes, its leaves stirring in the wind.

## 🍎 Orchard / farmland

- [x] **Crates**: stacked in piles, empty so they can hold anything.
- [x] **Apple (decor)**: a single small apple to hang in trees or fill crates and the wheelbarrow. It ties in with the apples you collect.
- [x] **Ladder leaning**
- [x] **Wheelbarrow**
- [x] **Scarecrow**: turns its head as you ride past.
- [x] **Beehive**: with a few pixel bees drifting around it.
- [x] **Tractor tyre**: half-buried in the ground, as a nod to the wheel in the logo.

- [x] **Prison fence**: chain-link, topped with razor wire.

## 🕷️ Spiders

- [x] **Spiderweb**: an orb web that trembles as you pass, its spider scurrying up out of the way.
- [x] **Cobweb**: a dusty sheet sagging from the roof, tatters stirring in the wind.
- [x] **Dangling spider**: lets itself down on a thread, and climbs back up when you come close.
- [x] **Cocoon**: wrapped in silk, swaying, and twitching now and then.

## 🏰 Dungeon

- [x] **Dungeon bars**: iron bars from the roof to the floor, a pair of them bent apart.
- [x] **Portcullis**: grinds up into the roof as you ride in, and drops behind you.
- [x] **Torch**: burns on an iron stand and lights the rock around it.

- [x] **Gibbet cage**: with its long-dead prisoner, swaying on its chain.

## 🌊 Water
*Goes with the water bodies.*

- [x] **Water lilies**: pads with a flower, riding the ripples.
- [x] **Reeds**: cattails on the bank or the bed, swaying in the breeze.
- [x] **Seaweed**: grows from the bed to just under the surface, swaying in the current.
- [x] **Fish**: swims to and fro, and darts off when the bike comes close.
- [x] **Duck**: floats and dabbles, and takes off when the bike comes close.
- [ ] **Frog on a lily pad**: jumps into the water as you pass.
- [ ] **Rowing boat**: moored, bobbing with the ripples.
- [ ] **Bubbles**: rise now and then from the bed.
- [ ] **Dragonflies**: hover over the surface on warm days.

## 🏁 Motocross track and event props
*These suit every level and make it feel like a trials event.*

- [x] **Tyre walls**: stacked tyres in different colours.
- [x] **Hay bale barriers**: placed along dangerous drops.
- [x] **Course tape and stakes**: strung between two points to mark the line.
- [x] **Sponsor banners**: made-up brands such as *Knobbly Tyres*, *Äpple Oil* and *Hjulben Racing*.
- [x] **Spectators**: cheer, or duck when you crash nearby.
- [x] **Pit board with a number**
- [x] **Petrol can**
- [x] **Marshal flags**: the start marshal waves a green flag, and a checkered flag is waved at the finish.
- [x] **Camera tower**: a small wink at the replay system.

## 🧭 Props that help gameplay
*Decoration that teaches the player without text.*

- [x] **Warning signs**: before steep drops or spikes, using a skull, a ⚠ or a "!" board.
- [x] **Arrow signs**: point the way on routes that double back or loop.
- [x] **Halfway flag**: lights up when passed and matches the split times.
- [x] **Graffiti-style tutorial drawings**: Showing the controls (lean back, lean forward, flip, light etc).
