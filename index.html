      RIDER HANDBOOK

      How to play

      01

      Gas and brake

      Hold ↑ or W to accelerate. Use ↓ or S to brake. On touchscreens, use the matching buttons below the game.

      02

      Shift your weight

      Use ←/A and →/D to lean. Move forward on climbs and back under hard braking.

      03

      Control the air

      Carry momentum into jumps and use small lean inputs to match the landing. Press Space to turn the bike around.

      04

      Finish the trail

      Collect every apple to unlock the checkered finish. Missing one? Turn around, find it, then return to the flag.
